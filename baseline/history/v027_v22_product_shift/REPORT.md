# V027 Result

V027 was implemented and tested as a v22-based market-only experiment. The
192-game development matrix rejected all three variants: V027a lowered paired
cash and win rate, while V027b/c never activated their mirror gate and had no
action differences from v22. All games were `DONE` with zero errors and
invalid actions. V22 remains the control and no V027 candidate is promoted.

See the full report and machine-readable gate result in
`baseline/artifacts/v027_v22_product_shift/REPORT.md` and
`baseline/artifacts/v027_v22_product_shift/gate_report.json`.
